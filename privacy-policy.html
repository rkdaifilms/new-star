Privacy Policy

New Star Creator Hub · Last updated: 19 November 2026

New Star Creator Hub (“New Star”, “we”, “our”) provides creator profiles, video publishing, messaging and related features. This page explains the categories of information the service may process. The operator must review and update this policy so it matches the services, vendors, settings and laws that actually apply before submitting the site to an advertising provider.

Information users provide

Depending on the feature used, this may include account and profile details, uploaded videos, captions, comments, messages, reports, support requests and information submitted in forms.

Information collected during use

The service and its infrastructure may process technical information such as device/browser details, log data, security events, approximate network information and feature usage. Location is used only when a user grants browser/device permission to a location feature.

Uploads and public content

Content a user chooses to publish publicly may be visible to other users and may be copied or shared by them. Private or unlisted features should be used according to the visibility choice shown at upload time. Do not upload personal information or content without the necessary rights and permissions.

Service providers

New Star uses hosted infrastructure for account, database and storage features. Depending on which features are enabled, video delivery and AI requests may be processed by configured service providers. Information sent to an AI feature should be limited to what is needed to answer the request. The operator must keep this list accurate and name the current providers and their applicable privacy documentation before launch.

Advertising and cookies

If advertising services are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies as described in their own policies and applicable law. Advertising must not be enabled until the relevant provider account is approved and the required privacy disclosures and consent controls are configured for the audiences and regions served.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and secure accounts, publish and deliver content, operate messaging, prevent abuse, respond to reports, provide support, maintain the service and meet legal obligations. Advertising or analytics uses must be disclosed before those features are enabled.

Security and retention

We use access controls and hosted security features intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Retention periods and deletion processes depend on the data type and operational/legal requirements; users should use the account controls or contact support to request help with their information.

Children and age-appropriate use

New Star must apply appropriate age safeguards and must not knowingly collect information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law. Parents or guardians can contact support with privacy concerns.

Your choices and requests

Users may update profile information and available privacy settings in the app. For access, correction or deletion requests, use the Support Inbox. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal or transaction purposes.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service changes. For privacy questions, open Contact & Support.
