What “own model” means

The repository includes a from-scratch experimental Transformer and training/inference code. It does not include a trained checkpoint. Chat, image and grounded research in this workspace use configured server-side AI providers until a trained New Star model is hosted and connected. Provider-generated images are not claimed as outputs of the New Star foundation model.

Never paste a provider secret into this page. Provider keys stay in Supabase server secrets. The own-model key is sent only to the HTTPS endpoint you enter.
