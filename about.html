About New Star Creator Hub

New Star Creator Hub is a creator-focused platform intended to help people build profiles, publish videos and connect with an audience.

What you can do

  • Create and manage a creator profile.
  • Publish original videos and choose available visibility settings.
  • Discover creators and interact through supported social features.
  • Use creator tools and monetization features when they are available and approved.

New Star does not claim ownership of user-uploaded content. Uploaders must have the rights and permissions needed to publish their material.
